## Introduction

Mini Kindle is a small, DIY e-reader project I built to carry my books around in the simplest way possible. It's a lightweight device that lets you load your own books and read them anywhere - no distractions, no notifications, just you and your book.

## Features

- 📚 Load and read your own books (EPUB / TXT / PDF)
- 🔋 Long battery life for distraction-free reading
- 🌙 Easy-on-the-eyes display
- 🪶 Compact and lightweight - fits in your pocket
